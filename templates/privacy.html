Data handling

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our top priority. Here's exactly how we handle your data: we don't keep any of it.

No Data Storage

DeepThink does not use a database, file storage, or any persistent storage mechanism. Your answers, demographic selections, and personality results exist only in temporary server memory during your active session, and are permanently deleted the moment your result is displayed.

No User Accounts

We do not require or support user accounts, login, signup, email collection, or any form of personal identification. You are completely anonymous.

No Tracking Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies, analytics pixels, or any third-party tracking services. The only cookie used is a standard Flask session cookie that contains a random identifier (no personal data) and expires when you close your browser or after 30 minutes of inactivity.

AI Processing

Your answers are sent to the Google Gemini API for analysis. This is a one-time API call that generates your personality report. We do not store the request or response beyond what's needed to display your result. Google's own data handling policies apply to the API call itself; we recommend reviewing Google's AI Terms of Service if you have concerns about AI processing.

Session Lifecycle

  • Session data is created when you start a test
  • It lives only in server memory (RAM), never written to disk
  • It is permanently cleared after your result is shown
  • It automatically expires after 30 minutes of inactivity
  • Closing your browser tab also effectively ends your session

What We Collect

Nothing permanently. During your session, the following is held temporarily in memory:

  • Age range (optional, e.g., "18-24")
  • Gender (optional, e.g., "Female")
  • Occupation status (optional, e.g., "Student")
  • Selected personality categories
  • Your question answers

None of this is linked to your identity, IP address, or any other identifier. After your result is shown, all of it is gone.

Not a Diagnostic Tool

DeepThink is for self-reflection and entertainment only. It is not a medical, psychological, or clinical diagnostic tool. If you are experiencing mental health difficulties, please reach out to a licensed mental health professional.

Contact

If you have any privacy concerns, feel free to review our source code. This is a fully transparent, open-source application with no hidden data collection.
